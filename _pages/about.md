# About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics at Case Western Reserve University(CWRU), advised by [Dr. Peter Thomas](https://case.edu/math/thomas/) and [Dr. Jake Scott(University of Rochester)](https://theorydi.vision/)

I am interested in using dynamical systems and stochastic analysis to study:
- circadian rhythms
- pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics (PK/PD)
- cancer resistance and evolution
- neurological disorders
- neuro-oncology

Before joining CWRU, I earned an M.S. in Math at the University of Minnesota, where I was advised by [Dr. Jasmine Foo](https://cse.umn.edu/math/jasmine-foo). I completed my undergraduate degree in Economics at the University of Toronto, where I did research with [Dr. Adam Stinchcombe](https://www.mathematics.utoronto.ca/people/directories/all-faculty/adam-stinchcombe) and [Dr. Kathleen Wilkie](https://math.torontomu.ca/~kwilkie/).
