# About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics at Case Western Reserve University(CWRU), advised by Dr. Peter Thomas and Dr. Jake Scott(Cleveland Clinic)

My research interests lie broadly at the intersection of **mathematical oncology, dynamical systems, and circadian rhythms**

Prior to joining CWRU, I received my M.S. in Mathematics from the University of Minnesota, where I was advised by Dr. Jasmine Foo. I completed my undergraduate studies at the University of Toronto, where I was supervised by Dr. Adam Stinchcombe and Dr. Kathleen Wilkie.
