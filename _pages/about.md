# About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics at Case Western Reserve University(CWRU), advised by [Dr. Peter Thomas](https://case.edu/math/thomas/) and [Dr. Jake Scott(Cleveland Clinic)](https://theorydi.vision/)

My research interests are broadly in mathematical biology. Because this is such a broad field, I think about my interests in terms of both the biological questions I am drawn to and the mathematical tools. On the biological side, I am particularly interested in cancer and neuroscience, including circadian rhythms, neuro-oncology, and neurological disorders such as Alzheimer’s disease. Within cancer research, I am especially interested in cancer resistance and cancer evolution. On the mathematical side, I am interested in dynamical systems and stochastic analysis.

Prior to joining CWRU, I received my M.S. in Mathematics from the University of Minnesota, where I was advised by [Dr. Jasmine Foo](https://cse.umn.edu/math/jasmine-foo). I completed my undergraduate studies at the University of Toronto, where I was supervised by [Dr. Adam Stinchcombe](https://www.mathematics.utoronto.ca/people/directories/all-faculty/adam-stinchcombe) and [Dr. Kathleen Wilkie](https://math.torontomu.ca/~kwilkie/).

I am grateful to Dr. Stinchcombe for introducing me to the world of mathematical biology and circadian rhythms, and to Dr. Wilkie for introducing me to mathematical oncology. Without their guidance, I might never have discovered mathematical biology or pursued it as my field of research.
