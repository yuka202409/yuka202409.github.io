# About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Applied Mathematics at Case Western Reserve University(CWRU), advised by Dr. Peter Thomas and Dr. Jake Scott(Cleveland Clinic)

My research interests are broadly in mathematical biology. Because this is such a broad field, I think about my interests in terms of both the biological questions I am drawn to and the mathematical tools I enjoy using to study them. On the biological side, I am particularly interested in cancer and neuroscience, including circadian rhythms, neuro-oncology, and neurological disorders such as Alzheimer’s disease. Within cancer research, I am especially interested in cancer resistance and cancer evolution. On the mathematical side, I am interested in dynamical systems and stochastic analysis.

Prior to joining CWRU, I received my M.S. in Mathematics from the University of Minnesota, where I was advised by Dr. Jasmine Foo. I completed my undergraduate studies at the University of Toronto, where I was supervised by Dr. Adam Stinchcombe and Dr. Kathleen Wilkie.
